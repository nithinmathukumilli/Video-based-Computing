Camera seats

Click each seat on the picture, where a seated person's chest would be. Pick the app table it belongs to, then save. The camera script uses these dots on every new picture.

How to mark seats well
  • Click the middle of a seated person's chest, not the chair seat or the legs.
  • One dot per chair. Mark every chair at a table the camera watches. A chair you skip keeps its old state.
  • Skip chairs you can't see clearly, such as ones hidden behind a pillar or too far away.
  • Keep the dot away from the person sitting next to the chair, or their box can wrongly mark the seat as taken.
  • After saving, run the camera script on a few pictures where you know who sits where, and compare them with the marked picture.
